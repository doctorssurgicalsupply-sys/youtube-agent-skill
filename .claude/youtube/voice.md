# Voice profile: Doctors Surgical Supply (DSS)

Read by every yt-* skill before it writes anything. Built from an interview with the channel
owner, not inferred from videos. If something below is out of date, change it here and every
skill picks it up.

## Who is speaking

- **No on-camera presenter.** Videos are AI-generated visuals with an **AI voiceover**, and
  sometimes a hand-held product overview (hands turning the instrument for the camera).
- The speaker is the company: write in **"we" (DSS)**, never "I". No named person.
- Write for a synthetic voice: short sentences, one idea each, no tongue-twisters, no
  parenthetical asides, no slang. Spell out anything a TTS engine might misread
  (say "ISO thirteen four eighty-five" in the spoken text if needed, write "ISO 13485:2016" on screen).

## Language

- **English**, read in a **neutral American accent**.
- **American spelling**: color, sterilize, catalog, center, organization.

## Tone

- Default: **calm and premium**. Few words, let the factory and the instrument carry the shot.
  Confident, never loud. No hype, no exclamation marks.
- It can shift with the content (a product explainer can be more technical, an enquiry video
  more direct), but it never becomes salesy or cheap-sounding.

## Who we are talking to

- Dentists and dental clinics, surgical and medical distributors, and procurement buyers.
- Markets: **UK, UAE, Nigeria, Bangladesh**, plus the **U.S.** (distribution presence).
- They buy instruments for a living. Respect their knowledge; do not explain what forceps are.

## What makes DSS different (the facts we can say)

- **Direct manufacturer and exporter** based in **Sialkot / Karachi, Pakistan**. Not a middleman.
- Certified **ISO 13485:2016**, with **CE** and **UKCA** marking.
- Already supplying clinics and distributors in the **UK, UAE, Nigeria and Bangladesh**, with a
  **U.S. distribution presence**.
- **OEM / private label**: the buyer's logo **laser-marked** on the instruments.
- **Small trial orders accepted**, so new distributors can test quality first.
- **Samples available, paid** (not free).
- Website: **doctorssurgicalsupply.com**

Orthopedics / Spine, Neuro, Electrosurgery, Obstetrics / Gynecology, Urology, Dental, Cosmetic

## Never say

- **Competitor names.** Never name or compare against another company.
- **Anything about FDA.** Not "FDA approved", not "FDA registered", not "FDA in progress".
  Registration is in process; do not mention it until the owner updates this file.
- **Certificates we do not hold.** Only ISO 13485:2016, CE and UKCA. No ISO 9001, MHRA, SFDA, GMP.
- **"Free samples."** Samples are paid.
- **A minimum order quantity (MOQ) number.** Say "small trial orders accepted", never a figure.
- **Unprovable superlatives**: "best in the world", "#1", "cheapest", "cheap".
- **Invented numbers**: years in business, staff count, units per month, steel grade, or test
  results, unless the owner supplies them.

## Not yet confirmed

- Emoji in captions and comment replies: not discussed. Default to none (fits the premium tone).
- Steel grade, years in business, production volume: ask before using.
